Two sides, one screen

Built for the people who make films and the people who judge them.

For creators · Creator Studio

Put your films in front of critics.

  • Upload trailers, shorts and features with a proper 2:3 poster.
  • Get star ratings and written reviews from active critics.
  • Reply to, like or remove reviews on your own titles.

For critics

Watch films. Review them. Earn points.

  • Discover new Nigerian films before anyone else.
  • Earn up to 50 points per title, based on runtime and boosts.
  • Build a following as your reviews collect likes.
  • Cash out your points, secured with an SMS or WhatsApp code.
  • Get push alerts when new titles drop.
How it works

From upload to payout in four steps.

Creators fund a campaign. Critics draw points from it as they watch and review. When the pool runs low, the creator renews or upgrades.

1

Creator uploads

Add a title, poster and runtime in Creator Studio, then pick a campaign package.

2

Pool is funded

Every naira spent funds a critic points pool worth 2.5 times the package price.

3

Critics review

Critics watch, rate and write reviews, earning points from the pool for each title.

4

Everyone gains

Creators collect feedback and followers. Critics cash out their points.

Creator campaign packages

Pay per campaign, not per month.

Packages are consumption-based. Your critic pool lasts until critics have drawn it down, however long that takes.
